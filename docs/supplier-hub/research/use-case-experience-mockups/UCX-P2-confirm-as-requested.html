Safeguards in this design. (1) Nothing is ticked at the start. (2) The review step repeats each quantity and date. (3) An order that needs a decision (escalated, a value limit, a change) cannot be ticked. (4) Undo stays open for 10 minutes; it needs a new verb, because no supplier act returns a confirmed order to Sent today. (5) Each order still runs its own confirm rule, so a limit stays enforced per order. The same bar would sit on Call-offs and on Foresight commitments, where "Submit all" already confirms a period at once.
