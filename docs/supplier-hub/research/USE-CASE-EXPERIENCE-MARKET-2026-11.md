# P2 evidence brief: simpler duties for the supplier portal

Run file of UCX P2, written 2026-11-21 in the evidence-grading format. Research folder: `research_notes/Supplier portal simplification evidence/` (three researcher notes). The Setting is PTI (PT Paragon Technology and Innovation), an Indonesian buyer, and its raw-material and packaging suppliers with mixed digital maturity. No source below studies that setting.

Grade note: every accepted source is peer reviewed, a preprint, vendor or official documentation, or practitioner writing. No vendor marketing page is used. Vendor documentation shows that a feature exists; it never shows that the feature helps. A status of Verified means I re-opened the anchor in this run and read the span again (abstract level where the row says so). Every other row is Unverified and rests on the researcher's note for its span. Eight anchors were re-opened (E-07 to E-10, E-21, E-22, E-24 to E-28). E-28 stays Unverified: it rests on one practitioner article, and no second source corroborates it (evidence-grading rule 5). Five of them needed a second route after the page fetch failed: the Europe PMC and NCBI interfaces for the PMC articles (the PMC pages return a bot check), and a local text extraction of the saved PDF for the two PDF papers. The Goddard and Hussain rows are verified at abstract level only.

## Question

Which interface patterns for action lists, waiting states, confirmation, shipment notices, weekly submissions, forms and inline help do accepted sources support for a supplier-collaboration portal, and where do they warn against simplifying?

## Answer

Accepted sources support a bundled clean-up of forms and error text that says how to fix the problem. One practitioner article, with no second source, advises keeping confirmations for irreversible acts. Vendors document one-action order confirmation, buyer-set tolerance gates and carry-forward of commitments, but no accepted source measures their effect on time or errors. A default or a recommendation moves choices even when a change costs one click, and training does not remove automation bias, so a prefilled or carried-forward value is not a safeguard by itself. No accepted source tests ranked, role-filtered action lists, confirmation of several orders at once, delivery-note scanning, prefilled defaults in business forms, or channel use among Indonesian suppliers. A proposal that rests on these gaps carries low confidence for its effect.

## Not found

- Ranked, role-filtered or deadline-ordered action lists, and opening the filtered record: no accepted source tests them. SAP Fiori pages returned HTTP 403, and no NN/g action-list article was found.
- The owner and next act in a waiting state: only the GOV.UK status labels (E-02) and one bundled guideline (E-10) touch it.
- Confirmation of several POs, schedule lines or call-offs in one action, and auto-acceptance inside a tolerance stated in words: no page read documents them. Coupa and Oracle Fusion PO pages were not read.
- A measured effect of confirm by exception, carry-forward or change-only submission: no non-vendor source. Two peer-reviewed CPFR leads surfaced and stayed unread.
- Prefilled values and smart defaults in business forms, a disabled submit button with no explanation, and a test of whether tooltips are read: no study found. One bundled guideline set and practitioner advice only.
- A lighter dispatch notice beside the ASN; how a portal generates ASN and delivery-note numbers; UN/EDIFACT directory text (HTTP 403); API or EDI connection, delivery-note scanning or OCR, and fit for small suppliers: nothing read beyond rows E-18, E-19 and E-20.
- Channel use (for example WhatsApp) and e-Faktur among Indonesian suppliers: not researched (budget). The first run on this topic stopped on the shared limit.
- Rubber-stamp approval in procurement, a test of highlighting what differs from a default, and a test of sample checks on bulk confirmations: no source.
- One name per concept: the source was still being read when the run stopped.
- A second source for undo, or for confirmations kept to irreversible acts: only one practitioner article (E-28) was found.
- Angles tried, by the researchers' own counts: ten searches for the interface-pattern run, seven for the supply-collaboration run and four for the run on evidence against simplification, all in standard mode, plus the re-open routes in the grading step.
